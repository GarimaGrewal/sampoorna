About me

Hi, I'm Rosy.

Your journey matters to me.

I'm a Holistic Life & Wellness Coach and Nutritionist, passionate about helping people reconnect with themselves and create a healthier, happier and more purposeful life.

I work with adults who feel stuck, overwhelmed, lacking direction, or are going through a life transition — helping them create positive change across mind, body and life. Having experienced life's challenges and transitions myself, I deeply understand the importance of clarity, self-belief and taking conscious action.

Through SAMPOORNA, I don't focus only on mindset or only on nutrition. I bring mind, body, habits and purposeful living together — so you can design a life that feels aligned, balanced and truly yours.
